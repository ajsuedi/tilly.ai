How the pieces connect

Read the workflow top to bottom. Each stage hands enriched context to the next, so nothing is re-collected and every downstream decision uses the fullest available picture.

  1. Capture — inbound and outbound channels create or match a record. No manual entry.
  2. Enrich — the record is auto-populated with org, person, signal and social data before a human ever sees it.
  3. Score and analyse — Tilly ranks conversion likelihood and overlays competitive position on the specific opportunity.
  4. Route — fit score plus deal complexity decides self-serve e-commerce or enterprise contract path.
  5. Engage — the personalisation layer pushes video, dossiers, proposals and contracts on the best channel.
  6. Escalate only when needed — the agent owns the journey until one of five defined triggers fires.
  7. Compete — outcomes feed the cockpit, where reps and products race on live leaderboards.
