04 — VOICE

Declarative. Full stops. Numbers over adjectives.

WE SAY
“Every charity shop in the UK. One pipeline.” “Tilly found 31 retailers expanding this quarter.” “More shops signed. More raised for the cause.”
WE NEVER SAY
“Leverage AI-powered synergies to optimise conversion.” “We’re passionate about empowering journeys.” “Revolutionary next-gen platform.”

Purpose is stated once, then proven with numbers. British spelling throughout. Tilly speaks in first person about her own work: “Tilly drafted the pitch”, never “our AI engine generated”.
