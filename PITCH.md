# Tilly — 5-minute pitch script (two speakers)

**Demo URL:** https://tilly-ai-git-main-tilly-ai.vercel.app/ · **Speakers:** Alina (prospecting, intel, self-serve) · Sian (enterprise, tender, customer intel, success)

> Before you start: open the URL in one tab, land on the Cockpit, close Ask Tilly if open. The DEMO bar switches surfaces at any time. Every number on screen is computed live — nothing is a mock-up screenshot.

---

## 0:00 — Cold open · ALINA · *(Cockpit on screen)*

**ALINA:** "Eleven thousand two hundred charity shops in the UK — and the companies selling to them are stuck with spreadsheets, because charity retail is famously the slowest market in Britain to adopt technology. Deals take months. Buyers need coaching, not pitching. So we built Tilly: the AI salesforce for charity retail. And because the market is slow, we made the CRM fast — this is a Formula 1 cockpit. Race pace inside the team, patience with the buyer."

*Point at the screen:* "This is the starting grid — everything needing a human this morning, tightest clock on pole. P1 is a customer who asked for a person. Nobody starts their day wondering what to do."

## 0:40 — Prospecting: the agent runs top-of-funnel · ALINA

*Click **Funnel** in the sidebar. Click **▶ Run the agent now.** Let it stream while you talk.*

**ALINA:** "Nobody on our team prospects manually. Watch Tilly work: overnight she polls filings, job ads, tender portals and grant registers. Right now she's fetching three charities, enriching them from the Charity Commission, scoring them… there — PDSA, fit 64, intent 58, Front Row. Crisis gets politely disqualified: eleven shops, below the sweet spot, reason coded. Two promoted to the pipeline, first-touch *education* drafted — never a cold pitch, because slow adopters buy from whoever teaches them."

## 1:20 — Intel: scoring you can argue with · ALINA

*Click **Pipeline**. Hover a **POLE** chip. Then click **▦ BOARD** and drag any card one column.*

**ALINA:** "Every account is scored twice — fit, who they are; intent, what they're doing — blended into a live likelihood. Hover any band and the model explains itself. Nothing here is a gut feeling: it's 0.4-fit, 0.6-intent, banded Pole to Cold." *Drag a card.* "Drag a deal and Tilly writes the audit entry herself. And the GTM funnel below self-serve tracks every ad pound — Google, Meta, LinkedIn — down to the exact step people drop off. Meta's leak? The card step on mobile, 71%. Tilly found it, and fixing it is one click."

## 2:00 — Self-serve: sign-up to subscription · ALINA

*DEMO bar → **SELF-SERVE SIGN-UP**. Step briskly: email → any 6-digit code → About → Company → Plan (flip the annual toggle) → Checkout.*

**ALINA:** "The self-serve motion is a full e-commerce funnel: two-minute signup, verification, per-seat pricing that reprices live, and a real checkout — VAT, seat count, trial-first, so a grant-funded buyer never pays before they see value. And when they land inside, a guided tour walks them through — because our buyers have never used anything like this. Sian, take them upmarket."

## 2:30 — Enterprise & tender · SIAN

*DEMO bar → **THE CRM** → Pipeline → open **British Heart Foundation**.*

**SIAN:** "Enterprise is where charity retail gets serious — 700 shops, formal procurement, committee decisions. Here's BHF. Top of the deal: exactly where it is and what to do next, with the clock. And this—" *point at the tender block* "—is the part no generic CRM has. The tender, found by Tilly on Find a Tender on day one, not day minus four. Notice ID, click-through to the source documentation, **23 days to deadline — counting down live**. The bid process tracked stage by stage, the six-test bid/no-bid gate checked in real time — mandatories 100%, scored coverage 78%, win-zone verdict — and every document, from the ITT to the 60%-drafted response, held under the deal."

## 3:20 — Customer intel: the people · SIAN

*Scroll to the stakeholder map, click **Amara Osei** (or Rachel Holt on Sue Ryder).*

**SIAN:** "Deals are people. Click anyone on the buying committee and Tilly gives you the person: contact details, what they've been posting, whether they're a promoter or a detractor — and an ease-of-doing-business score, so you know how hard this seat will be to win before you spend a month finding out. She even suggests the opener, built from their own words. And the guardrails are real: detractors and open complaints are blocked from automation entirely. Human only."

## 4:00 — Success: kept by an agent · SIAN

*Click **Success**.*

**SIAN:** "After the close, most CRMs go quiet. Tilly gets louder. Success runs on autopilot — onboarding nudges, upsells, recovery sequences, all run by the agent — and the red list is the entire human workload. Health is telemetry, scored nightly with the flag logic on screen. When risk lands, the safety car deploys: commercial motions freeze *first*, so no renewal email ever lands mid-complaint. The churn radar routes enterprise risk to a QBR and self-serve risk to a courtesy call — or a handwritten card in the post. And adoption gaps get fixed with a coaching video, not a sales call." *Click ▶ Preview video briefly.* "That's how a slow-adopting customer becomes a plus-seventy-five NPS advocate."

## 4:40 — Close · ALINA + SIAN

*Back to **Cockpit**. Ask Tilly: click the chip "How far am I from target?"*

**ALINA:** "One more thing — every rep has a race engineer. Ask Tilly anything and she answers with live numbers, or gets a human rather than guess."

**SIAN:** "Predictive scoring. Competitive verdicts. Next-best action. Auto-enriched records. Agents that act, not suggest. Not a contact database — a CRM that thinks ahead of the rep."

**ALINA:** "Tilly. Fast on our side of the table, unhurried on theirs. Thank you."

- Timing checkpoints: Sian should have the BHF dossier open by 2:35 and Success by 4:05.
